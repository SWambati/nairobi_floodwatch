How we use these sources

Data sources serve different purposes: some support historical model development, others provide operational weather updates, geospatial reference layers, or independent event verification. They are not all continuously queried by the live system.

ABOUT NAIROBI FLOODWATCH

Turning environmental data into meaningful flood awareness.

Nairobi FloodWatch is an academic flood-monitoring information system developed to explore how data engineering, machine learning, and geographic information systems can improve access to localized flood-risk information.

By combining historical environmental data, operational weather information, flood-event records, and geospatial analysis, the platform supports informed awareness of flood-risk conditions across Nairobi.

Explore Our Capabilities →
OUR PURPOSE

Make flood-risk information easier to access.

Provide residents and relevant agencies with understandable, geographically contextualized monitoring information.

OUR APPROACH

Connect data, predictions, and maps.

Bring environmental data acquisition, Random Forest classification, GIS analysis, and model-generated advisories together in one information system.

ACADEMIC PROTOTYPE

Supporting awareness, not replacing official warnings.

FloodWatch is a research and educational prototype. It is not an official emergency alerting service.

Important Notice

Nairobi FloodWatch is an academic prototype and is not an official flood-warning or emergency-response service. Flood-risk classifications and advisories are model-generated assessments and may contain uncertainty. They do not confirm that flooding is occurring at a specific location.

Always consult official advisories from the Kenya Meteorological Department and follow instructions from relevant emergency authorities.
